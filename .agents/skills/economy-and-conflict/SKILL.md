# Economy and conflict

Use for resource rates, trade, rewards, diplomacy, PvP, territory, protection, shelter, and scenario balance.

1. Read `docs/multiplayer-rules.md` and identify the exact rule being changed.
2. Trace sources, sinks, storage limits, travel time, risk, and recovery for the affected goods or power.
3. Evaluate incentives for solo, allied, aggressive, new, and temporarily absent players. Look for mandatory chores and dominant strategies.
4. Check repeated trades, alternate accounts, reconnects, simultaneous acceptance, protection cycling, alliance revocation, and loss farming where relevant.
5. Keep stakes bounded and understandable. Home protection and crew recovery cannot be bypassed by a different damage or ownership path.
6. Compare at least two plausible tuning configurations using a simple model or reproducible playtest. Mark unmeasured assumptions.
7. Update canonical rules/configuration, decision records, and acceptance checks together.

Output: incentive analysis, specific exploit cases, chosen working defaults, recovery behavior, and the next measurement. Never claim account abuse or economic balance is solved solely by a rule document.
